# Project Zomboid: Beginner's Guide

## Introduction

[Project Zomboid](https://store.steampowered.com/app/108600/Project_Zomboid/) is an open-world zombie survival game that challenges players to survive in a world filled with the undead. Unlike many survival games, Project Zomboid focuses heavily on realism. Players have to worry about finding food, managing injuries, staying healthy, and protecting themselves from zombies.

One of the things I enjoy about survival games is how every playthrough can turn out differently. In Project Zomboid, even a small mistake can completely change how long your character survives. There is no guaranteed way to win because the game is built around survival and learning from your experiences.

## Creating Your Character

Before starting a new game, players can customize their character by choosing traits, occupations, and skills. These choices affect how difficult survival will be.

Some useful skills and abilities to consider include:

- **Carpentry:** Helps with building and repairing structures.
    
- **First Aid:** Helps treat injuries.
    
- **Fitness:** Improves physical performance.
    
- **Strength:** Makes physical tasks easier.
    
- **Sneaking:** Helps avoid attracting zombies.
    

### Finding a Safe Location

Choosing a good starting location is important. A house with multiple exits, useful supplies, and access to nearby resources can make surviving much easier.

## Essential Survival Strategies

Food, water, and shelter should be your first priorities. Search nearby houses for supplies, but always be careful when entering buildings. Zombies can sometimes be waiting inside.

It is also important to avoid fighting large groups of zombies. Weapons can break, stamina can run out, and injuries can quickly become dangerous.

For more general advice, check out [[game-guides/survival-basics|Survival Basics]].

> In Project Zomboid, staying alive is more important than collecting everything you find.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

Many new players underestimate how dangerous zombies can be. Running through crowded areas, making too much noise, and carrying unnecessary supplies can create problems.

You can learn more about these problems in [[game-guides/beginner-mistakes| Beginner Mistakes]].

The most important lesson is to take your time, plan ahead, and remember that survival is a learning process.
