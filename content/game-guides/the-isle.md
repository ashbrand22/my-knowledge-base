# The Isle: Beginner's Guide

## Introduction

[The Isle](https://store.steampowered.com/app/376210/The_Isle/) is an open-world dinosaur survival game where players take control of different dinosaur species and try to survive in a dangerous prehistoric environment. The game focuses on survival, exploration, hunting, and interacting with other players. Depending on the dinosaur you choose, your experience can be completely different. Some dinosaurs are better at hunting, while others rely on staying hidden or traveling in groups.

One of the biggest challenges for new players is learning how to survive long enough to grow into an adult dinosaur. Food, water, stamina, and avoiding predators are all important parts of the experience.

## Choosing Your Dinosaur

Your dinosaur choice can make a big difference in how you play. Some species are better suited for beginners because they have more ways to avoid danger.

A few things to consider when choosing a dinosaur include:

- **Speed:** Faster dinosaurs have a better chance of escaping predators.
    
- **Strength:** Larger dinosaurs can defend themselves more effectively.
    
- **Diet:** Herbivores eat plants, while carnivores must hunt other animals.
    
- **Group size:** Some dinosaurs benefit from traveling with others.
    

### Learning the Basics

Before exploring too far, spend time learning how your dinosaur moves, runs, attacks, and interacts with the environment. Managing stamina is especially important because running constantly can leave you vulnerable when you need to escape.

## Survival Tips

New players should avoid unnecessary fights and pay attention to their surroundings. Listening for footsteps, watching for movement, and staying aware of nearby players can help prevent unexpected encounters.

I also recommend reading [[Survival Basics]] before attempting to explore dangerous areas.

> Surviving is not always about being the strongest dinosaur. Sometimes knowing when to hide or run is the better decision.

## Exploring the World

Exploration is an important part of The Isle. Players need to find reliable sources of food and water while avoiding dangerous areas. Learning the map over time makes it easier to find resources and plan where to travel.
